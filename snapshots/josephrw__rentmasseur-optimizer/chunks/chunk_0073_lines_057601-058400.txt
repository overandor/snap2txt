But seriously, being a great massage therapist has nothing to do with looks and everything to do with skill and attention to detail. So, even if I don't look like a Greek god, I can still make you feel like one.

## Q3: What's the weirdest request you've ever gotten from a client?
**A:** Okay, this is going to sound weird, but someone once asked me to massage their feet while they watched a video of cat plays on their phone. I mean, who am I to judge? It's all about relaxation, right? I've had clients request everything from singing to them (I'm no American Idol, trust me) to using specific types of essential oils. The moral of the story? I'm game for whatever – as long as it's not tickling, I'm a bit of a tickle-phobe.

## Q4: How do you deal with difficult clients?
**A:** You know, the ones who think they know more about massage than I do? I just smile, nod, and remind myself that I've spent years studying the art of massage, while they've spent years... well, not. But seriously, every client is different, and what works for one person might not work for another. I listen to their concerns, adapt my technique, and try to make them feel like they're getting the best massage of their life. And if all else fails, I break out the big guns: my extensive knowledge of dad jokes.

## Q5: What's the most common mistake people make when getting a massage?
**A:** Not communicating with me! I'm not a mind reader (although, that would be a great superpower). If you're feeling uncomfortable or the pressure is too much, just let me know. I want to make sure you're getting the most out of your massage, and that you leave feeling like a brand new person. Unless, of course, you want to leave feeling like a relaxed, gooey mess – in which case, I'm all for it.
